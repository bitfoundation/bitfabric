<p align="center"><b>Build with AI. Own everything.</b></p>

<p align="center">
  Your app starts full featured, not from an empty project: apps for every device, sign-in, an AI assistant and an

<p align="center"><a href="https://bitfabric.co">bitfabric.co</a></p>

---

### Privacy First
bit Fabric never sees your code, data or prompts.

### Zero Lock-in
The code, the data and the server are yours, and your app outlives us.

### Full featured from day one
Web, Android, iOS, Windows and macOS, with sign-in, an AI assistant, an admin panel and the rest already in place.

### Agents that work like a team
Agents that work like a seasoned product designer and a senior software engineer help you build a product that is
well designed, secure and solid: they ask what they need to know, plan the work, and review their own.

### Designed before it's built
The Workbench shows your pages on sample data, on every device, in light and dark, before they are built. You give
feedback right on the page.

### Live on your own server
New versions go live while your users keep working, on your own server and domain, securely and at a low cost.
